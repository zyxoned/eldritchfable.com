# Build the isometric MMORPG world

Start at the existing player's entry point, content tools and save model. In Eldritch Fable, use the authenticated `/play` game, its illustrated Canvas renderer, authoritative Node service and persisted party. Use the project's approved visual direction. Diablo Immortal and Baldur's Gate: Dark Alliance are references for camera intimacy, movement weight, dramatic light and readable action. Author original places, characters, art and sound.

When the work is a release-planning or acceptance request, use the [Eldritch Fable planning skill](../plan-eldritch-fable/SKILL.md). Implement its named slice within the frozen outcome and evidence criteria. For a new setting the default world brief is **eight overworld biomes, thirteen cities and six strongholds**, plus **The Underdark** and **The Maddening** as two additional explorable realms. In an existing setting, preserve established names and places. If an atlas or designer brief disagrees, record a designer decision and build the independent portions of the slice. The full atlas and worked mystery designs belong in the authorized private world-building brief.

## 1. Fix geography and expensive dependencies first

Maintain an atlas of stable IDs for biomes, cities, strongholds, roads, landmarks, clues, and both ends of each realm passage. Derive map and travel references from those records. Count a border settlement or stronghold once. Give each city a livelihood, goods, a service, and walkable approaches. Give every stronghold an objective and an exit after its state changes. Connect biomes in a circuit with cross-region shortcuts. Walk each road to a safe far-side arrival. Track each hero's visits and discoveries; reveal only earned destinations on their map.

## 2. Work backward from portal discovery

In the private authoring source, record what a player must learn to locate a gate and operate it. For each fact, give at least two credible sources that can be encountered in either order. Let physical evidence teach a solution when a guild, NPC or time window is unavailable. Make partial progress perceivable through motion, sound and a visual counterpart. Show characters what they personally saw or heard.

Keep fixed portal solutions, unrevealed lore and author-only routes in the authorized private source. Resolve dynamic puzzles on the server. Send each hero's earned cues, usable interactions and observed transitions in their own view. Test a companion arriving late, a member staying behind, either realm discovered first, individual crossing and independent return. Preserve earned discoveries through death and reconnect. Each realm needs an arrival, inhabitants, encounters and a usable return route.

## 3. Prove one expedition and its art in motion

Use the final camera angle, zoom, feet, door clearances and navigable area together. Join two genuine player sessions in the same party. Walk from a city's working service to a stronghold encounter, earn XP, equip a drop, use a newly unlocked skill, discover and cross a portal, and return. Capture gameplay through the normal entry. Keep a companion's travel voluntary.

For every visible hero, NPC and creature, use finished illustrated directional animation or fully rigged, richly modeled art consistent with the project's renderer. Reserve primitive shapes for invisible collision and navigation. Curate silhouettes, cloth and armor materials, weapon arcs, recognizable work motions and enemy anticipation at actual play zoom. Check foot contact, occluding roofs, hazard contrast and combat telegraphs on supported screens. Create a region kit with a recognizable landmark, architecture, terrain, ambient sound and weather or water response. Measure rendering on target hardware before scaling content production.

Plan the visual assets by scene, animation state, authoring source, reuse and expected budget. Assemble an in-game preview for every version. Eldritch Fable requires designer approval for each shipping asset; record IDs separately when several characters or scenes share one source file. Keep motion and audio cues legible with reduced motion or muted sound.

## 4. Connect work, equipment, classes and guilds

Give NPCs home and work anchors, travel and service hours, a visible task and a reason the output matters to the player. Advance distant routines from time and route state and restore them plausibly on arrival. Keep closed services discoverable and essential lore accessible through another route.

Use the game's authoritative progression records. Connect encounter participation, XP, character levels, skill ownership and cooldowns, inventory, equipment slots and observable effects. Show rarity through both text or symbols and color. Exercise simultaneous claims, retries, reconnect and class eligibility. Let player guilds invite, assign ranks and coordinate objectives. Give authored public and secret guilds distinct functions, discoverable invitations and meaningful exploration benefits. Limit a slice to its approved dependencies.

## 5. Expand after the expedition is playable

When the first integrated expedition passes, grow completed regions in waves of one, two, four and eight if capacity permits. Agree on route endpoints, trade flows and cross-region clue IDs before assigning regions independently. Start each realm interior once its entry and return contracts work. Recheck the dependents of changed IDs and preserve valid evidence for unaffected regions.

For the full-world release, walk all contracted cities and strongholds, reconcile the scene-backed counts, discover both realms in opposite orders, observe NPC cycles and guild initiation, exercise class progression and contested rewards, and inspect final art and performance in motion. For a single slice report its actual entry, implemented route, verified behavior, evidence and pending designer reviews. Track every fifth accepted game slice through the planner's designer playtest checkpoint.
